# Replication prompt — Project 15

Replicate the SPY SOTA time-series experiment with a reproducible local proxy. Generate an SPY-like OHLCV series with macro covariates, convert prices to log returns, create lagged predictors, preserve chronological ordering, and perform a walk-forward test. Compare a zero-return baseline with Ridge regression and compute directional accuracy, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown. Do not claim real-market performance from synthetic data.
